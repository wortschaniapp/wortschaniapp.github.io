Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Developer: Said Koca

This is an English translation for your convenience. If it differs from the Turkish original, the Turkish text prevails.

1. Who we are

WortSchani is a German-learning app developed and published by an individual developer, Said Koca, not by a company. In this policy, "we" refers to this developer.

The data controller for your personal data is this developer as a natural person.

2. No account needed

You do not need to create an account or give your name, email address or phone number to use the app. We do not ask you for identity details.

3. Information stored on your device

The words you learn, XP and streak, daily goal, placement test result, mistake list, grammar test scores and app settings are stored only on your device.

This information is not sent to our servers; we have no access to it.

4. Purchases

Premium subscription payments are taken through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). We never see or store your card or payment details.

To validate your subscription we use the subscription service RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). RevenueCat processes an anonymous user ID generated at random on your device, purchase and subscription details (product, transaction ID, dates, subscription status) and technical details such as app version, operating system and country. Your name, email address and payment details are not sent to RevenueCat.

This information is used to provide and validate the subscription and for aggregate sales statistics seen by the developer, never for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat’s privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy. Processing on the store side is governed by Apple’s and Google’s own privacy policies.

5. Read-aloud and reminders

Words are read aloud with your device’s own text-to-speech feature. The microphone is not used and nothing is recorded.

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device, and only if you allow them. No information is sent to a server for this and no notification ID is collected. You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings > Notifications or in your phone’s settings.

6. Ads

The free version of the app shows ads; Premium subscribers see no ads at all. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.). A full-screen ad may appear from time to time when a lesson ends; rewarded ads are watched only if you choose to (e.g. for extra words).

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google collects the following from your device: the advertising ID (IDFA on iPhone, advertising ID on Android) and app-scoped identifiers, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, the ads shown and your interactions with them, usage information such as app launches, and app performance and crash information. Your learning progress and settings are not shared with Google.

Personalized ads are shown only with your permission. On iPhone the app asks whether it may track you; if you decline, your advertising ID is not used. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent screen is shown first, and you can change your choices at any time in Settings > Support > Ad privacy choices.

You can also manage permissions in your phone’s settings: on iPhone, Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; on Android, Settings > Privacy > Ads (reset or delete the advertising ID). How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Apart from this, the app contains no separate analytics or tracking tool.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Because of the ads, we ask that children under 13 use the app only under the supervision of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Deleting data

You can delete the learning data on your device with Settings > Reset progress. Uninstalling the app also deletes all data on the device.

Your subscription is tied to your store account; deleting the app does not cancel it.

9. Your rights

Under Article 11 of Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, in the European Union (e.g. Germany), under the GDPR, you have the right to access your data, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, to object to its processing and to lodge a complaint.

You can send requests to wortschaniapp@gmail.com. As your learning data is kept only on your device, you can fulfil most requests yourself from within the app. For ad data you can change your choices in the settings described above, and you can also send requests about Google directly to Google.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the current version is published in the app and the "last updated" date is changed.

Contact: Said Koca · wortschaniapp@gmail.com
