Subscription and Refund Terms

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Developer: Said Koca

This is an English translation for your convenience. If it differs from the Turkish original, the Turkish text prevails.

1. Premium plans

1 Week: ₺49 / week · 1 Month: ₺119 / month · 3 Months: ₺249 / 3 months.

Prices may vary by country and currency. The price that applies is the one shown in the App Store or Google Play before purchase.

2. Payment

The charge is taken from your Apple ID or Google Play account when you confirm the purchase. Payment is collected by the store, not by the developer.

3. Automatic renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically for the same period and price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

The renewal charge is taken from your account within the 24 hours before the current period ends.

4. Cancellation

iPhone / iPad: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > WortSchani > Cancel Subscription.

Android: Google Play Store > profile picture > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > WortSchani > Cancel.

After you cancel, Premium stays open until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

5. Refunds

As payments are taken by the store, refund requests are also made to the store; the developer cannot issue refunds directly.

Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com · Google Play: play.google.com > Order history > Report a problem.

Statutory exceptions to the right of withdrawal may apply to contracts for digital content whose supply has begun immediately; the stores’ refund policies and your statutory rights as a consumer remain unaffected.

6. Restoring purchases

If you changed phones or reinstalled the app, you can reactivate your subscription on the same store account with Settings > Restore purchases.

7. Price changes

If the subscription price changes, the store informs you in advance and asks you to accept the new price where required.

8. Contact

For questions about your subscription: Said Koca · wortschaniapp@gmail.com
