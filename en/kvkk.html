KVKK Privacy Notice

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Developer: Said Koca

This is an English translation for your convenience. If it differs from the Turkish original, the Turkish text prevails.

1. Data controller

Under the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"), the data controller is Said Koca, the natural person who develops the WortSchani app as an individual.

2. Data processed

The app does not collect identity or contact details from you. Learning progress and settings are kept only on your device and are not transferred to the developer.

When you buy a subscription, the transaction details (transaction ID, plan, subscription status) are processed by the App Store / Google Play and, to validate the subscription, by RevenueCat under an anonymous user ID.

To show ads in the free version, Google AdMob processes the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions and usage and performance information. Personalized ads are shown only with your explicit consent (the tracking permission on iPhone, Google’s consent screen in the EU).

3. Purposes of processing

Running the app and storing your learning progress, providing and validating the Premium subscription, showing and measuring ads in the free version, and answering your requests and complaints.

4. Legal grounds

KVKK Art. 5/2-c (formation and performance of a contract), Art. 5/2-ç (compliance with a legal obligation) and Art. 5/2-f (legitimate interest of the data controller). The legal ground for personalized ads and the use of the advertising ID is your explicit consent (KVKK Art. 5/1), which you can withdraw at any time.

5. Transfers

Purchases are handled by Apple Inc. and Google LLC, which are located abroad; subscriptions are validated by RevenueCat, Inc. in the USA; ads are served by Google LLC (USA) and Google Ireland Ltd. This transfer takes place within the conditions set out in KVKK Art. 9 and only to provide the subscription service and to show ads. The developer does not transfer or sell your personal data to any other third party.

6. Method of collection

Data is collected electronically and automatically while you use the app, and through the transactions you make in the store.

7. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

You have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information about it, to learn the purpose of processing, to know to whom it has been transferred, to ask for it to be corrected if incomplete or wrong, to ask for it to be deleted or destroyed, to ask for these actions to be notified to those it was transferred to, to object to an outcome against you resulting from automated analysis, and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

8. Applications

You can send your applications to wortschaniapp@gmail.com. Applications are answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest.
