Copyright and Content Notice

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Developer: Said Koca

This is an English translation for your convenience. If it differs from the Turkish original, the Turkish text prevails.

1. Our principle

WortSchani respects the intellectual property rights of others and does not knowingly publish content that infringes them. If you believe your rights have been infringed, simply let us know as described below; we review notices promptly and free of charge.

2. Where the content comes from

Grammar lessons, example sentences, test questions, explanations, the design, the mascot "Schani" and the app texts were written by the developer.

The word lists, their Turkish meanings, the example sentences and their translations, and the plural and verb forms were compiled and written by the developer. Individual words, articles and grammar rules are common to the language.

If any content is nevertheless found to touch the rights of third parties, the section concerned is rewritten or removed.

The names of grammar topics (e.g. Präteritum, Passiv, Relativsätze) are general grammatical terms and do not belong to any publication.

3. Third-party components used

Software: React Native, Expo and related open-source libraries, used under their own licences (mostly MIT).

The rights to these components belong to their respective owners.

4. How to report a copyright infringement

Send your notice by email to wortschaniapp@gmail.com with the subject "Copyright Notice". Your notice should include:

• Your full name (or company name) and contact details,

• The work you believe you hold rights in and, if possible, where it was published (book title, web address, etc.),

• Where in the app the content you believe infringes appears: screen name, grammar topic, test and question number, or word,

• Information or documents showing that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them,

• A statement that you believe in good faith that the content is used without permission,

• A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and your name as signature (a typed name in the email is enough).

5. What happens after a notice

We confirm receipt of your notice by email within 3 working days at the latest.

We review the notice and may ask you for more information if needed.

If the infringement is confirmed, the content concerned is removed, changed or made inaccessible in an app update published within 7 days at the latest. You are told the outcome.

As the app content is on the device, the change takes effect when users update the app.

6. Counter-notice

If you believe content was removed by mistake or that its use is lawful, you can write to wortschaniapp@gmail.com giving your reasons. Counter-notices are reviewed with the same care.

7. Bad-faith notices

Untrue or bad-faith notices are not processed and may give rise to legal liability for the person who sent them.

8. User content

The app has no area where users can upload content. All content shown is provided by the developer.

9. Applicable law

This procedure was prepared with regard to Turkey’s Law No. 5846 on Intellectual and Artistic Works, Law No. 5651 on the Regulation of Publications on the Internet, and the copyright rules applied in the European Union (e.g. Germany). Your statutory rights remain unaffected.

You can also file through the app stores: Apple (apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/appstorenotices) and Google Play (support.google.com/legal) offer copyright notice forms.
